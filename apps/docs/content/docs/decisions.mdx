Some calls only a human should make: which of two approaches to take, whether
a destructive step is really wanted, what the workspace actually meant by a
vague issue. Instead of guessing or stalling, an agent can file a **Decision
Card** — a typed question with options — and continue once it has an answer.

## Decision Cards

A card carries a question, two to eight options (each an id, a label, and an
optional one-line impact), an optional recommended option, and an urgency
(`low`, `normal`, or `high`). A member can also file one by hand from the
issue.

A pending card appears in the issue's **Decisions** section with one button
per option — the recommended one leads — and a free-text alternative
("Answer with something else…") for when none of the options fit. A card
carries the reader's own answer history for that kind of question when it has
one ("You chose « Retry » 3 times out of 4 for this kind of request"); it is a
hint, not a default, and both stay a click away.

Answering is recorded once: a second attempt on the same card is refused.
When the issue is assigned to an agent, the answer starts one new run with
the question and the chosen answer at the top of its handoff note; a
human-assigned issue just keeps the recorded answer, since there is no run to
resume.

<Callout type="info">
If the workspace has set a Decision SLA (an admin setting), a card carries a
deadline. Past it, the card escalates — first to a designated substitute,
then to the workspace's owners and admins — and the card itself shows
"Overdue" with who was notified. Escalation only adds notifications; it never
answers the card.
</Callout>

## Requirement Interview

Before coding on a vague issue, an agent can ask up to three multiple-choice
questions at once instead of one at a time. They are Decision Cards sharing a
group, and the issue parks in a **Waiting for PM** status (created
automatically the first time a workspace uses this, in the `blocked`
category) while they wait. The run only resumes once every question in the
group is answered — one new run, with every question and its answer, in
order, at the top of its handoff note. Answering one question of the group
while a sibling is still pending changes nothing else yet.

An issue can only have one open interview at a time; asking again while one
is pending is refused.

## Where you answer

- **The issue itself** — the **Decisions** section lists every card on it,
  pending ones first.
- **Inbox → Decisions waiting for you** — up to five pending cards across
  every issue you can see, ordered like the [Attention Inbox](/inbox#needs-your-attention)
  (risk, then SLA deadline) with the total past five, answered inline without
  opening the issue.
- **Mobile** — the same list under **Inbox → Decisions**, answered with a
  swipe: right reveals "Answer `<recommended option>`", left reveals
  "Options" and opens the full list, and free text goes through a prompt.
  Nothing sends until you tap or confirm — the swipe only reveals the action.

Only a human can answer a card; an agent that tries is refused, the mirror
image of who is allowed to ask.

## CLI

An agent (or anyone scripting the API) files cards from the CLI; answering is
a human action and only happens from the UI.

```bash
multica decision ask <issue-id> \
  --question "Retry the failing deploy, or roll back?" \
  --option "retry=Retry|Keeps today's fix live if it works" \
  --option "rollback=Roll back|Safe, but loses today's fix" \
  --recommend retry \
  --urgency high
```

`--option` is repeatable and takes `id=Label` or `id=Label|impact`; at least
two are required.

```bash
multica interview ask <issue-id> --file questions.json
```

`questions.json` is `{"questions": [...]}`, one to three entries, each
`{"question": "...", "options": [{"id": "a", "label": "..."}, ...],
"recommended_option_id": "a"}` with two to eight options. `--file -` (or no
`--file` at all) reads from stdin instead of a file.

## API

| Method | Route | Actor | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/api/issues/{id}/decisions` | member | — |
| `POST` | `/api/issues/{id}/decisions` | agent or member | `{question, options[], recommended_option_id?, urgency?}` |
| `POST` | `/api/issues/{id}/decisions/{decisionId}/respond` | **human only** | `{option_id}` or `{modified_text}` — exactly one |
| `POST` | `/api/issues/{id}/interview` | agent or member | `{questions: [...]}` |
| `GET` | `/api/inbox/decisions` | member | the current user's up-to-five pending cards |

## Limits

- A card needs 2 to 8 options; each id must be unique, question at most 4 KB,
  each label or impact at most 512 bytes.
- An interview takes 1 to 3 questions, each with 2 to 8 options; only one
  interview may be open on an issue at a time (`409 interview_pending`).
- Answering an already-answered card returns `409 already_decided`.
- `urgency` is `low`, `normal`, or `high` (defaults to `normal`).
